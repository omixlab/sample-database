Benchmark against external reference tools

Each sAMPle model and its reference tool are scored on the same held-out peptides — a reproducible ~1,000-peptide sample of the model's own holdout split (never seen during its training), capped for practical external-tool runtime. A positive delta means sAMPle is ahead on this holdout; overlapping 95% confidence intervals mean the difference is not distinguishable from noise at this sample size. "vs. chance" is the model's own y-randomization null from its training run (open the model's row in the table above for the full record): a real signal, not just a win over one particular tool.
