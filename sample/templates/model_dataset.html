Holdout is scored on peptides never touched during training — the final word, on a single grouped train/holdout split (no cross-validation: hyperparameters are fixed, not selected). y-randomization refits on shuffled training labels and scores on the same holdout rows; a sound model's shuffled-label score should sit near chance.

Real vs. y-randomized null — significance

For each metric, a Shapiro-Wilk normality test runs on the real (bootstrap) and null (y-randomization) distributions first; the p-value used to decide significance is Welch's t-test only when both distributions are not shown non-normal, otherwise Kruskal-Wallis — the assumption-free test is trusted whenever normality does not clearly hold.
